ABCDE Self-Check Questionnaire

Work through each criterion of the clinical ABCDE rule. Answer honestly about any mole or skin spot you are monitoring.

A – Asymmetry B – Border C – Color D – Diameter E – Evolving
A – Asymmetry

If you draw a line through the middle of the mole, do the two halves look different from each other?

B – Border

Are the edges of the mole irregular, ragged, notched, or poorly defined?

C – Color

Does the mole have more than one color — different shades of brown, or any areas of black, red, white, or blue?

D – Diameter

Is the mole larger than 6mm across — roughly the size of a pencil eraser or larger?

E – Evolving

Has the mole changed in any way recently — size, shape, color, or symptoms like itching, bleeding, or crusting?
